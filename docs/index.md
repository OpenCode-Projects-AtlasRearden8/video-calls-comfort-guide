# Video Calls, Calm: A Soft Landing Guide

A calm, no-jargon manual for feeling *un-awkward* on video calls — for doctor visits, family
check-ins, club meetings, and chats with the grandkids. Nothing here will make you feel
silly for not knowing something. Everyone started where you are now.

This guide was written like a friend sitting next to you, pointing gently at the screen.
It complements the **Family Tech Support Guide** (that one is for the person who gets
called on Sunday to fix things; this one is for the person in front of the camera).

## What's inside

1. [Gear: camera and mic on any budget](01-gear.md) — including the "$0, your phone is enough" tier
2. [Framing and light tricks](02-framing-light.md) — look polished without buying anything
3. [Captions and how to turn them on](03-captions.md) — captions help nearly everyone, not just a few
4. [Muting manners](04-muting.md) — how mute works, when to use it, and the polite ways to handle it
5. [Screen sharing without leaking your tabs](05-screen-share.md) — the single most useful privacy trick in this whole guide
6. [AI note-taker bots, consent, and how to say no](06-ai-note-takers.md) — plus ready-to-copy wording for both sides
7. [Grandkid call setups](07-grandkid-calls.md) — making the calls actually fun, for all ages
8. [Troubleshooting echo and frozen squares, kindly](08-troubleshooting.md) — fix problems without blaming anyone
9. [The 2-minute pre-call checklist](checklist.md) — printable
10. [The family dial-in card](dial-in-card.md) — one page with everything the family needs, printable
11. [Glossary](glossary.md) — plain-English words for all the jargon

## How to use this guide

- **Right before a call?** Jump straight to the [checklist](checklist.md). It takes two minutes.
- **Family keeps missing calls?** Fill out the [dial-in card](dial-in-card.md) and share it.
- **Everything else** is here whenever you're curious. Skim, don't sprint.

## A promise up front

- Nothing in this guide requires buying anything.
- Nothing here requires any new account, sign-up, cloud service, or subscription.
- Nothing here involves anyone's personal data being collected. The built website in
  `site/` is plain HTML files — you can open it right off your own computer, offline,
  with a web browser, and it phones home to no one.

That's the whole spirit of this guide: local-first, warm, and yours.

## The one sentence worth memorizing

> Your camera is your chair, your light is your lamp, and your mute button is your good
> manners. Everything else is optional.
