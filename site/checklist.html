The 2-Minute Pre-Call Checklist

Print this page (Ctrl/Cmd + P) or keep it open in a tab. It's deliberately short — a pre-call routine should be doable on the day of the visit, not the day before.

The checklist

Tech (30 seconds)

  • [ ] Plug in / charge up. A phone at 12% is a countdown on your attention.
  • [ ] Headphones or earbuds on, if you have them — best echo insurance there is.
  • [ ] Check sound: play a quick video, or use the app's mic test at Settings → Audio.
  • [ ] Check picture: look at your own preview; wave test from chapter 2 — hand in frame, chin in frame, both lit.

The room (60 seconds)

  • [ ] Camera at eye level. Stack of books. Truly, it's the whole trick.
  • [ ] Light in front of you, not behind. Face the window; lamp at 10 o'clock.
  • [ ] Glance behind you — anything you'd rather not be taped to the fridge?
  • [ ] Close the big uploads/downloads (backups, streaming shows) so the call keeps the family's internet to itself.

The call itself (30 seconds)

  • [ ] Which app, whose link — confirm with the dial-in card if you're calling family.
  • [ ] Know where the mute button is and where the captions button is (chapter 3) before you need either.
  • [ ] If you'll share your screen, open the one window you plan to share and remember: share the window, never the desktop (chapter 5).
  • [ ] If a note-taker bot might show up, decide your one-liner — asking consent or declining — from chapter 6.

Last, the human bit

  • [ ] Water within reach. Glasses on top of head? Retrieval mission complete.
  • [ ] Set the timer for how long you'd like the call to be. Good calls end on time.

Stuck on any of these? Each line links back to the chapter that explains it — that's the whole reason this guide is organized the way it is.
