Video Calls, Calm: A Soft Landing Guide

A calm, no-jargon manual for feeling un-awkward on video calls — for doctor visits, family check-ins, club meetings, and chats with the grandkids. Nothing here will make you feel silly for not knowing something. Everyone started where you are now.

This guide was written like a friend sitting next to you, pointing gently at the screen. It complements the Family Tech Support Guide (that one is for the person who gets called on Sunday to fix things; this one is for the person in front of the camera).

What's inside

  1. Gear: camera and mic on any budget — including the "$0, your phone is enough" tier
  2. Framing and light tricks — look polished without buying anything
  3. Captions and how to turn them on — captions help nearly everyone, not just a few
  4. Muting manners — how mute works, when to use it, and the polite ways to handle it
  5. Screen sharing without leaking your tabs — the single most useful privacy trick in this whole guide
  6. AI note-taker bots, consent, and how to say no — plus ready-to-copy wording for both sides
  7. Grandkid call setups — making the calls actually fun, for all ages
  8. Troubleshooting echo and frozen squares, kindly — fix problems without blaming anyone
  9. The 2-minute pre-call checklist — printable
  10. The family dial-in card — one page with everything the family needs, printable
  11. Glossary — plain-English words for all the jargon

How to use this guide

  • Right before a call? Jump straight to the checklist. It takes two minutes.
  • Family keeps missing calls? Fill out the dial-in card and share it.
  • Everything else is here whenever you're curious. Skim, don't sprint.

A promise up front

  • Nothing in this guide requires buying anything.
  • Nothing here requires any new account, sign-up, cloud service, or subscription.
  • Nothing here involves anyone's personal data being collected. The built website in site/ is plain HTML files — you can open it right off your own computer, offline, with a web browser, and it phones home to no one.

That's the whole spirit of this guide: local-first, warm, and yours.

The one sentence worth memorizing

Your camera is your chair, your light is your lamp, and your mute button is your good manners. Everything else is optional.
