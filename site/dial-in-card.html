The Family Dial-In Card

One page that answers the two questions that sink the most family calls: *"which app?" and "which link?" Fill it in once, share it with everyone, or print it and stick it on the fridge next to the emergency numbers. It's the video-call equivalent of the phone-list card that used to live by the kitchen wall.

Copy this into a message to your family (or print this page and fill it in by hand — the checkboxes are just decoration there; leave the blanks for pen).

Our family video-call card

Our standing call is: ☐ FaceTime ☐ Zoom ☐ Google Meet ☐ Teams ☐ WhatsApp ☐ Other: ______

It happens on: day: ____________ time: ____________ (and whose time zone: ____________)

Everyone in the call book

Who Which device Their ID (phone / email / username)

The meeting details (Zoom/Meet/Teams)

  • Meeting ID / room name: ________________________________
  • Link (if any): _________________________________________
  • Passcode (if any): _____________________________________
  • Who hosts: _____________________________________________

If it doesn't work, plan B is:

  1. Try ____________ instead (a different app, or a plain phone call).
  2. If video fails, switch to sound only — turn the camera off; the visit can continue beautifully on voices. See troubleshooting.
  3. Last resort: call this number: ______________ (that's ______________'s phone).

House rules we all agreed on

  • ☐ Captions on by default (chapter 3)
  • ☐ No recording, or ask before any note-taker bot joins (chapter 6)
  • ☐ Calls end while they're still sweet — short and warm beats long and tired
  • ☐ Frozen screens are nobody's fault; keep talking, we'll hear you

Why this tiny card works so well

  • It removes the "wait, which app?" scramble that keeps older relatives from dialing in on time — and keeps younger relatives from starting three different calls.
  • Plan B means a frozen screen is a two-minute detour, not an abandoned visit.
  • The house rules mean decisions (captions, bots, call length) get made once, calmly, instead of every call, from scratch.

Warm advice: fill it in once with the family, then never argue about apps again.

Print, fill, fridge. Done.
